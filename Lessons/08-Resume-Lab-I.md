## Overview: Why This Still Matters (10 min)

A resume that survives 2027/28 hiring has to clear two different readers: an ATS (Applicant Tracking System — the software that scans and keyword-matches resumes before a human sees them) keyword pass, and then a human who's skimming for 20 seconds. Neither of those readers is impressed by a resume that "looks nice" — they're both looking for specific, checkable claims. Today and in [Resume Lab II](10-Resume-Lab-II.md), you're not polishing prose, you're auditing evidence.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of today, you will be able to...

1. **Audit** your current resume against concrete, specific criteria — not "does it look nice"
1. **Rewrite** weak resume bullets into outcome-driven ones
1. **Identify** gaps in your portfolio that a hiring manager or an ATS would flag

## Warm-Up: Resume Self-Audit (10 min)

**Alone, before opening the full checklist:** score your *current* resume 1-5 on each:

- Every bullet has a number or a concrete outcome, not just a responsibility
- It's one page
- You could defend every skill listed, in an interview, right now, with no notice

Where's your weakest score? That's likely where to spend the most time today.

## Lab Time: Resume & Portfolio, Part 1 (1 hour)

Work through the **Resume** section of the [Resume, Portfolio & LinkedIn Checklist](../Assignments/Resume-Portfolio-LinkedIn-Checklist.md). Your instructor will meet with you 1-on-1 during this time — keep working independently until it's your turn.

## Break (10 min)

## Peer Check-In (15 min)

With a partner: share your top 2-3 rewritten bullets. Partner gives one piece of feedback using the checklist's own test — does this bullet show an *outcome*, or just a responsibility?

## Lab Time: Resume & Portfolio, Part 2 (40 min)

Work through the **Portfolio** section of the [checklist](../Assignments/Resume-Portfolio-LinkedIn-Checklist.md). 1-on-1 meetings continue.

## Debrief (10 min)

As a class: what's one resume myth you believed before today that the checklist corrected?

## Exit Ticket (5 min)

**Students:** individually, no partner — submit via the [Exit Ticket form](https://forms.gle/hLLsHySp2BT7qXAZ7) before you go.

> What's the single weakest bullet or section on your resume right now, and what specifically will you do to fix it before Wednesday?

**Instructor:** if several students name the same weak spot (e.g. "my bullets don't have numbers"), that's worth a 2-minute whole-class callout at the start of Lesson 10 rather than repeating the same 1-on-1 note to everyone individually.
