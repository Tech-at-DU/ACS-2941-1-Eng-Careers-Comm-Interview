# Behavioral Question Bank

Standard behavioral interview questions, grouped by category. Use this for the "cold question" portion of a mock interview — pick one the interviewee hasn't specifically prepped for.

## Teamwork

- Tell me about a time you worked closely with a team to get something done.
- Describe a time you had to rely on someone else to finish your part of a project.
- Tell me about a time you helped a teammate who was struggling.

## Conflict

- Tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate or manager. How did you handle it?
- Describe a conflict you had on a project and how it got resolved.
- Tell me about a time you had to push back on an idea you thought was wrong.

## Failure / Mistakes

- Tell me about a time you failed or made a significant mistake. What did you learn?
- Describe a project that didn't go the way you planned.
- Tell me about a time you had to admit you were wrong.

## Initiative

- Tell me about a time you took initiative without being asked.
- Describe a time you identified a problem before anyone else noticed it.
- Tell me about something you built or improved that wasn't part of your assigned work.

## Learning Quickly

- Tell me about a time you had to learn a new technology or skill fast.
- Describe a time you were out of your depth on a project. How did you catch up?

## Prioritization / Pressure

- Tell me about a time you had to manage competing deadlines.
- Describe a time you had to deliver something under significant time pressure.
- Tell me about a time you had to say no to a request because of your workload.

## Leadership

- Tell me about a time you led a project or a group, even informally.
- Describe a time you had to motivate a team member who was disengaged.

## Communication

- Tell me about a time you had to explain something technical to a non-technical person.
- Describe a time feedback you gave (or received) was hard to deliver or hear.

## Why This Role / Company

- Why are you interested in this role?
- What are you looking for in your next opportunity?
