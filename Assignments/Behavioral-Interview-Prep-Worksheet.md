# Behavioral Interview Prep Worksheet

Make a copy of this file (or use a doc of your own) and fill it out. Keep it nearby as reference material while practicing — the goal is to have your stories ready, not memorized word-for-word.

## Your Stories Bank

List 5-8 real experiences (projects, jobs, internships, group work, even non-technical roles) that you can pull from. For each one, note:

- What was the situation/task?
- What did *you specifically* do (not the team)?
- What was the result? Use numbers if you have them.
- What would you do differently now?

## STAR Answers

Pick your 4-5 strongest stories from the bank above and write them out in full STAR form:

- **Situation** — brief context, 1-2 sentences
- **Task** — what you specifically were responsible for
- **Action** — what you did, step by step (this is the longest part)
- **Result** — the outcome, ideally with a measurable impact, plus what you learned

Map each story to the common question categories below — most stories can answer more than one:

- Tell me about a time you worked on a team
- Tell me about a conflict with a teammate or manager
- Tell me about a time you failed or made a mistake
- Tell me about a time you took initiative without being asked
- Tell me about a time you had to learn something quickly
- Tell me about a time you disagreed with a decision
- Tell me about a time you missed a deadline or had to manage competing priorities
- Tell me about a time you used an AI tool to solve a problem faster or better — what did you check or change before trusting the output?

## Your "Why This Company / Why This Role" Answer

Draft a 30-60 second answer for "Why do you want to work here?" that you can adapt per company. Note 2-3 things you'd actually change per employer (product, mission, tech stack) so it doesn't sound generic.

## Questions to Ask the Interviewer

Prepare 3-5 questions. Avoid anything answered on the company's website. Good categories: team structure, how AI tools fit into their engineering workflow, what a new grad's first 90 days look like, how the team handles code review/feedback.
